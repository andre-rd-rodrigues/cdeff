## VI Funchal Futsal Cup International Tournament

It had the participation of 60 teams, from various clubs, distributed by 5 levels, of which: U-11, U-13, U-15, U-17 and U-19, involving a total of 940 athletes, of which 650 came from abroad, coming from mainland Portugal, Spain, France, Hungary, Ireland and Italy.

It was considered the biggest Futsal Tournament in Portugal in 2023, with the most games.
