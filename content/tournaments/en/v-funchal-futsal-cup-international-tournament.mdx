## V Funchal Futsal Cup International Tournament

It had the participation of 30 teams, from various clubs, distributed by 5 levels, of which: U-11, U-13, U-15, U-17 and U-19, involving a total of 550 athletes, of which 260 came from abroad, coming from mainland Portugal, Spain and France.
