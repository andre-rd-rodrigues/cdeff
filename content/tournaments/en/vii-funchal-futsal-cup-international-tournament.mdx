## VII Funchal Futsal Cup International Tournament

Our 7th Funchal Futsal Cup International Tournament is aimed at the U-11, U-13, U-15, U-17 and U-19 age groups, with the main objective of celebrating unity and friendship between different cultures and nationalities that share a common passion, futsal.

This tournament is scheduled to take place from 2 to 8 July 2024 in order to reduce (excessive) downtime between sports seasons, start player promotions for 2024/2025 and reward the annual dedication of our players and coaches.

The Funchal Futsal Cup International Tournament has already had 6 previous successes, which show the quality and competence of all those involved in this organisation, as reported by all the participants who have already experienced this great event.

Take advantage of this unique experience to get to know what our marvellous island of Madeira has to offer and be part of a community where the atmosphere overflows with joy and the team spirit is second to none. With teams from all over the world, unmissable tourist activities and lots of fun, you won't want to miss this opportunity.
